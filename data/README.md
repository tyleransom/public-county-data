Here is an overview of the different data sources, what they contain, and their provenance:

| dir                       | vars                                                   | source                                                            |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| air-pollution             | NOX, Ozone, etc.                                       | EPA                                                               |
| bea                       | total employment                                       | BEA                                                               |
| bls-laus                  | unemp rate                                             | BLS-LAUS                                                          |
| census                    | pop, demographics                                      | ACS 5yr                                                           |
| coastline                 | coastal dummies                                        | Census                                                            |
| county-adjacency/cleaned  | adjacent counties list                                 | Census                                                            |
| elevation                 | elevation at centroid                                  | USGS                                                              |
| health                    | obesity rate, many other health outcomes (smoking rate, teen birth rate, mortality, …) | County Health Rankings & Roadmaps |
| mines                     | geocoded mine locations                                | Homeland Infrastructure Foundation-Level Data (HIFLD) from the US Dept of Homeland Security |
| oil-and-gas               | geocoded oil & gas well locations                      | Homeland Infrastructure Foundation-Level Data (HIFLD) from the US Dept of Homeland Security |
| qcew                      | sectoral employment shares and earnings                | BLS                                                               |
| temperature               | Jan/Jul temp, precip                                   | Wolfram Schlenker’s version of the PRISM data                     |
| vote                      | dem vote shr in pres elections (interpolated)          | MIT Election Lab                                                  |
| watershed                 | HUC boundaries                                         | hand coded by Tyler Ransom after consulting various maps          |
